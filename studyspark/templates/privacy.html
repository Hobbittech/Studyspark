What we store

  • Your account: username, email, and a securely hashed password (we can never see your real password).
  • Your study sets: the notes you paste and the material generated from them, so you can find them later in your library.
  • Usage counters: how many generations you run per day, to enforce plan limits.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data. Ever.
  • We never show your notes or study sets to other users.

AI processing

Your notes are sent to an AI provider (currently Groq) solely to generate your study materials, and are not used to train AI models (per provider API terms).
